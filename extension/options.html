Booru Favorites Highlighter

Point the extension at your offline favorite archives. It reads the post IDs and matches them while you browse.

1  Archive folders

Add the folders where your downloaded favorites live — e.g. D:\boorus\rule34 favorites and D:\boorus\gelbooru danbooru favorites. The extension only reads folder names and the small info text files; nothing leaves your computer.

Your browser blocked folder access. Use the Import option below instead.

    2  Scan

    Scans are saved automatically inside the extension and remembered across browser restarts, reboots, and extension reloads. You never need to back anything up for normal use.

    3  Backup & import (optional — not needed for normal use)

    Only useful for moving your list to another PC, or keeping a copy in case you ever fully uninstall the extension. Export your scanned IDs as JSON, or import one (e.g. built by the included scanner script) if you'd rather not use in-browser folder access.

    Settings for display (colors, hide/remove mode) live in the toolbar popup.
